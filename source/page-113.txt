The adverbof time“从来”indicates that somethinghas always been the case from the
past to the present.It is often used in negative sentences and sometimes in affirmative
sentences.For example:
（1）爱，从来都是相互尊重、理解与支持，不是吗？
（2）我从来没见过这个颜色的花，它叫什么名字啊？
（3）我从来不喜欢一个人旅游，每次都要和家人或朋友一起去。
